Cities • Municipalities • Tourism Boards

Turn dog parks from a complaint source into a service.

A city or tourism pilot can combine dog-park mapping, clear rules, safety information and visitor guidance in one mobile-first interface.

1. Whitelabel city map

A proposed pilot can provide a branded dog-park map for your website using verified local data and a lightweight interface.

  • City logo, colours and language.
  • All public dog parks, off-leash areas and „no go“ zones clearly marked.
  • Mobile-first interface for locals & tourists.

2. Rules, signage & education

A pilot can present local rules and dog-park guidance in plain language for residents and visitors.

  • Dog-park etiquette and safety tips.
  • Clear explanation of local rules (leash, hours, restricted areas).
  • Multilingual support for tourist regions.

3. Data & feedback loop

Where sufficient verified feedback exists, aggregate signals can help identify recurring issues such as shade, fences, bins or water access.

  • Aggregate feedback summaries where enough reliable data exists.
  • Simple indicators: crowding, cleanliness, safety.
  • Integration options can be scoped separately during a pilot.
