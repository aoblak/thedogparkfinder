Understand the downside before you unclip the leash

Risk guidance is general context, not a current inspection report for a specific place.

Crowding

Stress and conflict risk can rise when dogs lose space and owners stop tracking behaviour.

Check the actual situation before entering.

Terrain and boundaries

Holes, debris and weak fencing can turn a normal sprint into an injury or escape.

Walk the boundary before unclipping.

Size and play style

A safe interaction depends on more than intent; body size, speed and play style matter.

Leave when the mix is wrong for your dog.

Food and toys

High-value resources can create avoidable conflict in enclosed spaces.

Keep the environment simple when several unfamiliar dogs share it.
