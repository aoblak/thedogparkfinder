Advertise with us

Reach people who actually walk their dogs.

The Dog Park Finder connects dog-related businesses with people actively looking for parks and dog-friendly places. Advertising inventory is currently limited and handled as a controlled pilot.

Local Essentials

For vets, groomers & local shops

Pilot local placements can put your business alongside relevant park and city content without turning the site into an ad wall.

  • Business placement on selected relevant pages.
  • Link to your website, social profile or map listing.
  • Simple monthly placement summary where measurement is available.
  • Limited local inventory to reduce clutter.

Trainer & Walker Spotlight

For trainers & walkers

A pilot profile can present your services near locations where owners are already thinking about exercise, behaviour and training.

  • Profile information, approach and contact details.
  • Placement near selected parks in your service area.
  • Specialty tags such as puppies, reactive dogs or sports.
  • No fabricated ratings or paid ranking disguised as editorial content.

Regional & Brand Pilots

For brands & larger chains

Regional campaigns can be designed around selected cities, regions or park content with a dog-first, privacy-conscious format.

  • Geographic placement by agreed city, region or park set.
  • Seasonal campaign concepts such as summer heat or winter care.
  • Optional content collaboration subject to editorial review.
  • Aggregate reporting only where reliable measurement is available.

For current pilot availability and pricing, send your city/country and business type to ads@thedogparkfinder.com. We keep placements limited: no flashing banners, no autoplay video and no undisclosed paid ranking.
